# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of constructive feedback.
Unacceptable behavior: harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to **si.sibomana@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
